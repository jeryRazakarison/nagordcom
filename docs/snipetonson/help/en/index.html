Snipe your sound

Help and frequently asked questions

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Snipe your sound is a soundboard for iPhone: your favorite sounds on the main screen, one tap and it plays. A question that isn't here, a sound that went wrong, an idea? Write to contact@nagord.com, or use Settings > Contact the team in the app.

How do I choose the sounds on the main screen?

Open the sound bank with the tab at the bottom, then tap the star on a sound: it joins your favorites, which become the buttons on the main screen. Tap the star again to remove it. The main screen shows 3 buttons, and up to 6 with Snipe Pro.

I can't hear anything

Turn the volume up with the buttons on the side of your iPhone, and check that no headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected: the sound may be playing there. If the problem continues, close the app from the app switcher and open it again.

What is Sniper mode?

The sound you picked plays by itself as soon as you open the app: handy to fire without looking for the button. Turn it on in the app's settings. To choose the sound, touch and hold a sound, then tap “Set as Sniper sound”.

How do I set a trap?

Touch and hold a button on the main screen, tap “Set a trap”, then choose the delay. The sound goes off by itself when the countdown ends. The app has to stay open and on screen: put your iPhone down and walk away.

How do I add the widget?

On your iPhone's Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap “Edit” then “Add Widget”, and search for Snipe your sound. The “Favorite sounds” widget shows your first three favorites and plays them with one tap, without opening the app.

How does a group session work?

One person, the Master, starts a session: tap the antenna on the main screen, then “Start a session”. The others open the app on their iPhone, tap the antenna, then “Join a session”. Every sound the Master plays then goes off on all the phones.

Sessions work between nearby iPhones over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with no Internet and no account. Starting a session requires Snipe Pro; joining is free.

I can't find my friends' session

The first time, your iPhone asks for access to the local network: you need to allow it. If you declined, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network and turn on Snipe your sound. Also check that Wi-Fi or Bluetooth is on and that the phones are close to each other.

What does Snipe Pro unlock?

Up to 6 buttons on the main screen, the 7 Pro themes, your choice of app icon, an extra sound pack and starting group sessions. Two options give the same access: a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

I bought Snipe Pro but it isn't active

Open the Snipe Pro page in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

How do I cancel the subscription or get a refund?

The subscription is managed by Apple: iPhone Settings app > your name > Subscriptions. Refund requests are made at reportaproblem.apple.com: Apple handles the payments and decides on refunds.

Where do the sounds come from?

Every sound was made for the app, generated or synthesized. There are no clips from movies or shows, and no real person's voice.

What about my data?

No account, no ads. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in its settings. All the details are in the privacy policy.
