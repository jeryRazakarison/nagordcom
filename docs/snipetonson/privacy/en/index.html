Snipe your sound

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Snipe your sound never asks for an account, a name or an email address, and shows no ads. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, hosted in the European Union, to learn which sounds and features people enjoy. You can turn them off at any time in the app settings. Your favorites and settings stay on your phone.

Who publishes the app

Snipe your sound (Snipe ton son) is an iPhone app published by Nagord (Jery Razakarison). For any question about this policy, write to contact@nagord.com.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads, and no advertising tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • No data is sold or shared for commercial purposes.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which sounds are played most and which features are actually used, the app sends usage statistics to PostHog, an analytics service whose servers used here are located in the European Union.

What is sent:

  • actions taken in the app: sound played (and from where: home screen, sound bank, widget or Sniper mode), favorite added or removed, theme, icon or number of buttons chosen, Snipe Pro page opened, Snipe Pro purchased or restored (the kind of offer, never your payment details);
  • app installs and launches;
  • technical details: device model, iOS and app version, language.

These statistics are tied to an identifier randomly generated on your phone, specific to the app. It does not contain your name, your email address or the device advertising identifier, and it disappears when you delete the app. As with any web connection, PostHog sees the IP address of your connection, but it is not stored.

To stop sending them: app Settings > Share anonymous usage statistics. Once turned off, nothing is sent anymore, including on later launches.

Your favorites and settings

Your favorite sounds, their order, the theme, the icon and the other settings are stored only on your device. The home screen widget reads what the app prepares for it on the same device. None of this is sent anywhere, and everything is erased when you delete the app.

Purchases

Snipe Pro is bought through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: Nagord never receives your name, email address or banking details. The app simply checks with the App Store, on your device, whether Snipe Pro is active.

Contacting us and rating the app

“Contact the team” opens an email to contact@nagord.com, prefilled with the app and iOS versions. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message, which are used only to reply to you. “Rate Snipe your sound” opens the app page on the App Store, whose use is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app asks for no personal information and is not specifically intended for children under 13.

Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you, among others, the right to access, object to and erase your data. You can object to the statistics at any time from the app settings. Since they are tied neither to your name nor to your email address, Nagord cannot find the data of a specific person. For any question, write to contact@nagord.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, this page will be updated with a new date.
