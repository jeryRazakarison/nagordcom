1€ le Café

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1€ le Café does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no usage analytics and no tracking. If you allow it, your location is used only on your phone, and your stamp card stays there too.

Who publishes the app

1€ le Café is an app for iPhone, iPad and Android published by Nagord (Jery Razakarison). If you have any question about this policy, email contact@nagord.com.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No usage analytics and no tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK built in.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Your location

The app may ask for access to your location, only while you are using it. This is optional.

  • Your location is used only to sort cafés from nearest to farthest and to estimate walking time.
  • This is computed on your phone: your location is never sent to Nagord or to any third party.
  • Without location access, the app works just the same and lists cafés by arrondissement instead.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time in your device's Settings (iPhone, iPad, Android).

Your stamp card and preferences

Your stamp card (the cafés where you have already had your coffee) and your preferences are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere, and they are erased when you delete the app.

Network connections

When it starts, the app downloads the public list of cafés from framacarte.org, which hosts the collaborative uMap map “Cafés à 1 €”. This request contains no personal data; as with any web request, the host can see your connection's IP address.

The base map depends on your device:

  • on iPhone and iPad, it is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit) and covered by Apple's privacy policy;
  • on Android, map tiles come from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), which receives your IP address and the map areas being displayed, but not your location.

Directions and sharing

The Directions button opens your phone's maps app (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android). The Share button opens the system share sheet. Both happen only when you tap them, and whatever the app you choose does next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you, among other things, the right to access and erase your personal data. Since 1€ le Café collects none, Nagord has nothing to look up or delete on any server: whatever the app keeps lives only on your device. For any question, email contact@nagord.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, this page will be updated with a new date.
