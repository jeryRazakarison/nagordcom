1€ le Café

Help and FAQ

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1€ le Café lists the Paris cafés where an espresso still costs one euro. The app is available on iPhone, iPad and Android. Can't find your question here? Email contact@nagord.com.

What is the app for?

Finding a €1 coffee near you in Paris. Cafés are shown on a map and in a list, sorted by walking time if location is on, or by arrondissement otherwise. For each café you can see the conditions (at the counter, to go…) and the hours of the offer, and get directions.

Where do the addresses come from?

From the collaborative map “Cafés à 1 €” hosted on Framacarte, fed by readers of paris.fr and by social media. In the app's About section, “Open the original map” takes you to it.

The price or conditions don't match

The information comes from a collaborative map, so prices, hours or conditions may have changed since. The simplest thing is to ask at the counter before ordering.

What does “Valid now” mean?

This filter keeps only the cafés whose €1 offer applies right now. It always uses Paris time, even if your phone is set to another time zone.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to sort cafés from nearest to farthest and estimate walking time. This is computed on your phone and your location is never sent to anyone. It's optional: without it, cafés are listed by arrondissement.

How do I turn location on or off?

  • On iPhone and iPad: Settings > 1€ le Café > Location, then choose to allow access while using the app, or never.
  • On Android: Settings > Apps > 1€ le Café > Permissions > Location (labels may vary by manufacturer).

When location is off, the app also shows a Settings button that takes you to the right screen.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The list of cafés is built into the app and updated every time it starts with a connection available. Without network, the app shows the list it already has and says “offline”; the base map may then be incomplete.

Where is my stamp card stored?

Your stamp card keeps track of the cafés you have stamped. It is stored only on your phone: it is not sent to any server, and it is erased if you delete the app.

Report a closed café or a price change

Email contact@nagord.com with the café's name and address and what has changed. Thank you, it helps everyone.

What about my data?

1€ le Café does not collect any personal data. All the details are in the privacy policy.
